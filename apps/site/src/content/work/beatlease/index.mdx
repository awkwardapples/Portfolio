A platform I co-founded during the final year of my BSc, for music producers to sell their beats,
and the lease options on them, to their own clients through their own website: secure, automated
sales and delivery, with dashboards and SEO. It set out to be a lower-cost alternative for customers
who felt existing platforms neglected their needs.

Part of my role was consulting directly with clients: I gathered their non-technical feedback and
turned it into technical work, for me or for another developer. I enjoyed that direct contact with
clients. The rest was full-stack development, with Remix, PostgreSQL, Stripe Connect for payments and
Cloudflare R2 for storing and delivering the files.
