A platform I am building for music producers to sell their beats, and the lease options on them,
through their own website: secure, automated sales and delivery, with dashboards and SEO. It is built
with Remix, PostgreSQL, Stripe Connect for payments and Cloudflare R2 for storing and delivering the
files.
