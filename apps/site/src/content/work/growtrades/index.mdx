I started GrowTrades in 2026: a small digital marketing agency that handles the digital side of
local trades businesses, so their owners can be more selective about the jobs they take. Each client
gets a lead-generation site built to turn local searches into qualified leads, an instant quote
wizard that gives customers a price guide and sends the owner the job details, local SEO including
their Google Business Profile, automated replies to Google reviews that we approve, analytics that
compare before and during the service, and an automated lead flow, with support around the clock.
Inside the agency, AI agents automate the work, so onboarding is the only regular manual upkeep.

The agency works only if client results justify a monthly retainer, new clients go live quickly
enough for the margin, and many small sites can be maintained without the cost growing with every
client. That is why the software is one engine configured per client rather than a site built from
scratch each time. SCB Handyman Services in Guildford is the first client: this is their live site,
with the quote wizard I built, and the pipeline behind it.

## What it does

A homeowner chooses a service, answers a few questions about the job, and sees an estimated price
range straight away; services that need pricing by hand send a quote request instead. Each request
becomes a row in the business's Google Sheet and a WhatsApp message to the owner.
