A mathematical paper I wrote delving into the theoretical foundations of neural networks, machine
learning, deep learning scalability, and optimisation algorithms. The paper provides a simple neural
network topology and builds on principles established in a simple example to expand understanding to
deep learning. The paper aims to provide a good entry point for academics looking to gain a broader
technical understanding of machine learning techniques.

It is an expository paper and has not been peer-reviewed.
