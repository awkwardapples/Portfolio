A rapid full-stack demo built for a generative AI hackathon on agentic risk assessment: an AI agent
that assesses the risk of working with a vendor. Choose a vendor and the agent reads its profile (whether
it processes personal data, its certifications, where its data is kept and its incident history),
reasons over it with the OpenAI API, using retrieval-augmented generation to pull in the data it
needs, and returns a risk level, a recommendation, a confidence and an explanation.

In the recording, a vendor with SOC 2 certification that keeps personal data in the EU is approved
automatically, while one that processes personal data in the US without certifications is escalated
to the security team.

I presented it as a consulting-style investor pitch to generative AI experts in London, and it later
inspired my entry to the Open Agent 2026 hackathon.
